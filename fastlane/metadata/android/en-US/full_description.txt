Vehicle Forge is a free, open-source Android vehicle diagnostic app.
Read generic SAE OBD-II trouble codes, explore live sensor readings and export privacy-conscious reports with a compatible Bluetooth Classic ELM327 reader. Use the built-in simulator without a car or adapter.

Features:
• Read-only fault code retrieval and basic live data
• Offline code explanations and saved CSV reports
• Bluetooth ELM327/Vgate support where compatible
• Safe synthetic diagnostic and coding simulations

Limitations:
• Not all vehicles, ECUs or adapters are compatible.
• ECU coding, flashing and security access are not implemented.
• No guaranteed fault diagnosis or repair outcome.
• Work on parked vehicles you own or are authorised to diagnose.

No advertising, tracking or account requirement.
Source code: https://github.com/damunozx123-debug/vehicle-forge-x
